How to round image corners

  1. Add your image. Upload, drag or paste it. It opens with medium rounded corners.
  2. Pick the radius. Use the slider or the presets: Small, Medium, Large or Circle.
  3. Download a PNG. The rounded corners are transparent, so the image sits cleanly on any background. A JPG fills them with white instead.

Make a circle picture

For a round profile picture or avatar, open the Square tab and choose “Around subject” or “Whole image”, then choose “Circle” in the Corners tab. On a square image, the largest radius gives a perfect circle; on a rectangle it gives a pill shape. You can also make the image square first on its own page.

Where rounded corners look good

  • Slides and documents: rounded screenshots and photos look softer and more modern.
  • Websites and app mockups: match the rounded style of cards and buttons.
  • Profile pictures and logos: a circle crop with a transparent background works on any site.
  • Stickers: remove the background, add a color and round the corners for a sticker-style image.

Frequently asked questions

Are the corners transparent?

Yes, in the PNG download. JPG doesn’t support transparency, so its corners become white.

How is the corner radius measured?

As a percentage of the image’s shorter side: 50% is the maximum, which makes a circle on a square image and a pill shape on a rectangle.

Can I remove the background as well?

Yes. Press “Remove background” and the AI cuts out the main subject. You can then keep it transparent or choose a color in the Background tab.

Is it free? Is my image uploaded?

It’s free with no account or watermark. Everything happens in your browser, so your image is never uploaded.
